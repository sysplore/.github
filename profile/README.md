## What we build

- **Sysplore Mail** — email, calendar and contacts on your own domain, from **$3 per mailbox / month**. Webmail, IMAP, POP3 and SMTP, spam and phishing filtering, and encryption at rest to a key you control.
- **Sysplore Workspace** — files and folders with sync, document collaboration, calendar, contacts and unlimited audio and video calls, from **$5 per seat / month**.
- **Sysplore Full Suite** — both products on one seat, one bill and one admin console, from **$5.36 per seat / month**.

No ads, no profiling, and no training on your content. See [products](https://sysplore.com/#products) for details and availability.

## What we do

We design, operate, secure and modernize the infrastructure behind business applications.

- **Managed Hosting** — we run and maintain your production applications: updates, backups, monitoring, patching and incident response.
- **Infrastructure Management** — servers, networks and systems: monitoring, patching, backups and ongoing maintenance.
- **Security and Compliance** — assess the gaps, implement the technical controls, and prepare the evidence your auditors and customers ask for.
- **Digital Transformation** — custom software, open-source ERP, workflow automation and integrations that connect the systems you already use.
- **AI Engineering** — AI systems that integrate with your applications and data, with control over where models run and what they can access.

## Open source

We run a stack we did not write. Where we ship a modified version of a project, our changes are published under that project's licence.

Some of what runs Sysplore:

- **Platform** — k3s, containerd, etcd, CoreDNS, Caddy, Argo CD, Pulumi, Prometheus, Rancher
- **Mail and collaboration** — Stalwart, Bulwark, Roundcube, Nextcloud
- **Data and identity** — PostgreSQL, CloudNativePG, Valkey, Keycloak
- **Web** — Python, FastAPI, Uvicorn, Jinja, Pydantic, Tailwind CSS
- **Business applications** — ERPNext, Frappe CRM, Frappe Helpdesk

See the [full list](https://sysplore.com/open-source) with each project and its licence. If we have credited a project wrongly or missed one, write to support@sysplore.com.
