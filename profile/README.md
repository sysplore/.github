## Sysplore Mail

Business email on the domain you already own, from **$2.99 per mailbox / month**.

- **Open standards, not lock-in.** IMAP, POP3, SMTP, JMAP, CalDAV and CardDAV — Outlook, Apple Mail, Thunderbird or any client your team runs.
- **You hold the keys.** Encryption at rest with an OpenPGP key or S/MIME certificate you register; the private key stays in your client and never reaches us, so we cannot read what it protects.
- **Spam never reaches you.** Every message is scored at SMTP time — a classifier that learns from your own spam marks, plus blocklists (Spamhaus ZEN, SURBL), phishing heuristics and sender reputation. You decide what is quarantined and what is rejected.
- **Mail your recipients trust.** SPF, DKIM with automatic key rotation, DMARC, ARC, and delivery over DANE and MTA-STS, set up on every domain.
- **Priced per account, not per feature.** Unlimited mailboxes, custom domains, aliases and catch-all addresses on every plan. You only pay for storage (30 GB or 200 GB pooled) and sending limits.

Sieve scripting, two-factor authentication, webmail, calendar and contacts included. EU or US data region, chosen at sign-up. Export your mail any time — your export rights stay with you. [Details](https://sysplore.com/products/sysplore-mail).

## Sysplore Workspace

Files, documents and meetings in one place, from **$5 per user / month**, with 200 GB of storage per user.

- **Every app on every plan.** Documents, spreadsheets, presentations and PDFs edited together in the browser; notes, tasks, boards and tables; whiteboard; forms; workflow rules for the repetitive work.
- **Meetings for up to 50 on camera**, more watching, for as long as you like — in the browser, on the desktop or on the phone.
- **Share a link that expires.** Password and expiry date on every shared link, with control over what the recipient can do.
- **The standards your clients know.** WebDAV, CalDAV, CardDAV and JMAP; single sign on with the identity your other systems use.

Same features on every plan — you only pay for storage. Enterprise adds from 1 TB per user and a named contact on a dedicated channel. EU or US data region, exportable whenever you leave. [Details](https://sysplore.com/products/sysplore-workspace).
